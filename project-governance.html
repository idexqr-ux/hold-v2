Project Governance

Project record

Statement of intent

Hold keeps understanding connected to evidence and people.

The public demonstrations show how evidence can remain connected to its source, how uncertainty can stay visible and how people retain judgement and responsibility.

Project Governance

Assist understanding. Do not decide.

Hold can bring forward relevant context and explain the limits of what is known. People remain responsible for review, judgement, approval and action.

Public demonstration principles

What visitors should expect to see

Evidence

Demonstrated understanding remains connected to the records and context used to support it.

Provenance

Visitors can see where information came from and distinguish source records from assisted explanation.

Uncertainty

Limits, gaps and matters requiring confirmation remain visible rather than being presented as settled fact.

Human judgement

Hold supports people in understanding what matters; it does not authorise decisions or take operational action.
