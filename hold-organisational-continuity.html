Evidence

Demand varied sharply between teams, referral routes were inconsistent, and customers repeated information during hand-offs.

Inference

A central triage function would route routine and specialist work more consistently.

Assumption

Regional teams and the specialist partner could absorb peak volumes without reducing local responsiveness.

Conclusion

Adopt central triage with regional delivery cells and specialist partner cover.

Disagreement

The Service Quality Lead cautioned that central triage could lose local context for complex cases.

Uncertainty

Combined demand, partner unavailability and workforce absence had not been tested.

Conditions under which people acted: introduce the model in phases, retain regional escalation, review after twelve months, and reconsider if customer accessibility or complex-case performance deteriorated.
