# Hold Content Use Policy

The Hold demonstration site is provided for human review, evaluation, discussion and potential commercial enquiry.

Visitors are welcome to read and evaluate the Hold demos.

However, the content, structure, wording, concepts, examples, interface language, scenarios and explanatory materials on this site may not be used to train AI models, build AI training datasets, generate synthetic training data, fine-tune models, benchmark commercial AI systems, or otherwise improve AI products without prior written permission from IdexQR.

AI crawlers, training crawlers and automated systems acting for model-development purposes are not granted permission to use this content.

Search indexing for ordinary discovery is permitted where it does not involve AI model training.
