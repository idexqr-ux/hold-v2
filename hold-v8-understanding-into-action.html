What Changed Operationally

Consequences observed after action
Fleet capability

  • FLT-04 was the primary dispatch asset.

After

Maintenance position

Before

  • FLT-04 had an increasing reactive maintenance pattern.
  • 27 interventions had been recorded.

After

  • FLT-11 entered a planned maintenance programme.
  • A new preventive maintenance schedule is active.
Evidence
People readiness

Why It Matters

Hold shows whether a decision produced the intended operational change.

Read more
  • The action trail connects approval, procurement, receipt, training and record updates rather than treating them as unrelated tasks.
  • Missing follow-through becomes visible—for example, an asset can arrive while training or QR-linked information remains incomplete.
  • The organisation retains both the understanding that justified the action and the evidence of what happened afterwards.
